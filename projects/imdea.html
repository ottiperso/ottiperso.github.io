Project Overview

As a Computational Fluid Dynamics Engineering Intern at IMDEA Energy's High Temperature Processes Unit, I contributed to Spain's national green hydrogen initiative, an effort to meet decarbonization objectives for climate neutrality.

My work focused on developing thermal energy storage technologies to optimize renewable electricity to hydrogen conversion efficiency. Specifically, I modeled latent thermal energy storage (LTES) systems using phase change capsule materials to improve the performance of hydrogen production by high temperature electrolysis.

Research Focus

The project addressed a challenge in green hydrogen production: optimizing the connection between renewable electricity generation and electrolyzer operation through thermal energy storage. My CFD work focused on:

  • Fluid Flow Dynamics: analyzing how heat transfer fluids move through phase change capsules
  • Melt Fraction Trends: tracking how phase-change materials transition between solid and liquid states, modeling conduction and convection inside the capsule
  • System Efficiency: evaluating overall performance of different configurations to increase electrolyzer efficiency

My Contributions

  • Analyzed capsule characteristics to identify optimal geometries for heat transfer efficiency
  • Compared phase change materials to identify optimal materials for heat transfer efficiency
  • Validated CFD models against experimental data and theoretical predictions
  • Provided data and recommendations for capsule design in a final department wide presentation
  • Contributed findings to a departmental research paper on green hydrogen systems

Technical Approach

Simulation Software: Used CFD fundamentals and COMSOL software to build the phase change capsule computational models.

Analysis Parameters:

  • Geometric comparisons of different capsule configurations (cylindrical, spherical, etc)
  • Material analysis for various phase change materials
  • Melting and solidification behavior, thermal gradients
  • Overall system efficiency
