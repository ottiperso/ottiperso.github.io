Fleet of Test Rigs, Subsequent Analysis

I designed, built, and operated a fleet of 12 reflector-membrane tensioning and characterization rigs. I was responsible for the mechanical design, electronics, ESP32 firmware, and a wireless dashboard. I used them to characterize how reflectors hold tension and how they respond over a sine sweep of frequencies.
Hardware, firmware, and data/analysis pipeline, end to end.

Goal

The goal was to characterize the reflectors (spring rate, first & second mode frequencies, flatness vs tension relationship, reflectiveness metrics via CV) and inform the controller theory of the reflector in orbit.

Project Overview

Reflect Orbital is building a constellation of satellites with large deployable reflectors to deliver sunlight on demand after dark.

As a Mechanical Engineering Intern on the Deployer Team, I built and ran the test infrastructure used to characterize the reflector membranes themselves: how they hold tension, how they respond dynamically, and how accurate their optical surface figure is.

Membrane characterization is very important for our mission - to inform our operations and controls in orbit. In turn, these will determine the reflectivity of our mirror and ultimately if we meet our lux requirement.

My Contributions

  • Rig Fleet 1: Designed, built, and operated a fleet of 4 reflector-membrane tensioning and characterization rigs
  • Rig Fleet 2: Scaled to and built a fleet of 8 reflector-membrane tensioning and characterization rigs for a different reflector application
  • Embedded Control: Wrote ESP32 firmware with PID tension control, load-cell feedback, frequency-sweep drive, automated tests, safety interlocks
  • Wireless Dashboard: Built wireless GUIs for live monitoring and control across the rig fleets
  • Membrane Dynamics: Led automated sine-sweep and spring-rate campaigns on reflectors to characterize membrane dynamics
  • Optical Metrology: Built a Raspberry Pi 5 deflectometry instrument for optical surface-figure measurement of reflectors, with a computer-vision analysis pipeline
  • Test Satellite Parts: Designed, built, and printed motor adapters such that our satellite's test motors could interface with flight motor brackets.
  • Cross-Team Comms: Interface with avionics, GNC, deployer, mechanical teams to gather data and align on needs from testing (organized effort via Confluence live doc).

Technical Skills

  • Closed-loop PID control with load-cell force feedback (with deadbands, filters, creep bands)

  • Python data analysis (resonance: phase/amplitude analysis, spring rate plots, etc)
