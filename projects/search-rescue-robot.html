Mission Overview

The R3D5 robot system was designed to respond to simulated natural disaster scenarios inspired by Hurricane Katrina.

The integrated robotic system combines a UR5 robotic arm and custom mobile robot to locate and rescue a simulated casualty ("Tim") from a disaster scene.

The system navigates unstable terrain, locates Tim using IR sensors, removes debris with an electromagnet, extracts Tim with a custom gripper, transfers him to a mobile robot's swaying crib, and transports him to safety.

UR5 Gripper Design & Control

Custom Dual-Servo Gripper

  • 3D-printed claws and UR5 mount designed for Tim's dimensions
  • Integrated IR sensor, electromagnet, and web(sky)cam
  • Careful operation, monitoring Tim's "health status" for a secure hold without crushing

Controller Features:

  • Manual gripper open/close and electromagnet toggle
  • Pre programmed sequences: descent/pickup, roof removal
  • Singularity prediction to prevent arm locking

Mobile Robot Design & Control

Mechanical Design:

  • 192mm Wasteland Wheels for speed and obstacle clearance
  • Swaying crib with dowel suspension and funnel design minimizes impact on casualty
  • Reduced chassis width for tight space maneuverability

Custom Controller:

  • Dual joysticks: one for forward/backward, one for turning
  • Moving average low-pass filter smooths joystick inputs
  • Hardware speed mode switch for high and low speed modes
