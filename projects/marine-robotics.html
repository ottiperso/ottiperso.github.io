Algaenaut ROV - MIT Portugal Marine Robotics Summer School

Location
Horta, Faial, Azores
When
July 2025
Marine Robotics ROV Design Ecosystem Monitoring

Project Overview

Selected as 1 of 12 MIT post-graduate students for the MIT Portugal Marine Robotics Summer School (MRSS) held in the Azores islands (off the coast of Portugal).

This intensive program combined theoretical lectures with hands-on fieldwork, focusing on marine robotics, oceanography, and ecosystem monitoring.

MRSS brought together students, professors, field experts, and the local community to address environmental challenges through robotics and ocean technology.

The Challenge

The Azores islands face a growing problem with invasive algae species that threaten local marine ecosystems.

Our team decided to design and operate an ROV capable of tracking and documenting invasive algae populations around Faial Island, providing important data for conservation efforts.

My Contributions

  • Collaborated with an international team of engineers and scientists
  • Assembled and tested baseline ROV system: programmed basic functionality and connection to cameras
  • Adjusted the fins and system buoyancy to improve the ROV's maneuverability and stability
  • Added and integrated a pressure sensor, temperature sensor, and location tracker (GPS only when above water)
  • Tested and operated the ROV around the island with my team, collected algae video data
  • Helped build a Niskin bottle ROV attachment (to collect algae samples)
  • Analyzed collected data: algae coverage based on camera recordings, algae species identification based on samples
  • Co-presented our findings to the MIT Portugal team, Okeanos department, and Escola do Mar instructors

Results & Impact

Successfully deployed and operated the ROV in multiple locations around Faial Island, collecting valuable data on invasive algae distribution. The project proved the feasibility of using affordable, custom built ROV systems for environmental monitoring in remote locations.

Technical Details

ROV Capabilities:

  • Depth rating suitable for coastal algae monitoring
  • HD and fish-eye camera system for underwater documentation
  • Real time video feedback to surface
  • Stable control system for precise maneuvering
