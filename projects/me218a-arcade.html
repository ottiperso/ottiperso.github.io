Game Concept

Dungeon Attack is a whack-a-mole inspired game that involves three lanes. The goal is to attack zombies and collect as much treasure as possible along the way. Each lane randomly flips up either a zombie or a treasure chest. Players must move to the correct lane using a joystick and respond with the appropriate action (wave hand over IR sensor or press attack button).

Gameplay Mechanics

Scoring Points:

  • Players score points by moving into the correct lane and responding appropriately (kill zombies or collect treasure)
  • Earn 500 points to advance to the harder level where zombies take two hits to defeat

Losing Lives:

  • Players start with three lives and reacing incorrectly costs a life
  • Zombie health is shown with blue lights above each lane

Difficulty Levels:

  • Three game modes: Easy, Medium, and Hard (turn up speed dial to increase difficulty)

Technical Implementation

Hardware Components:

  • Three lanes with pop-up mechanisms that used a 180º servo with a 2:1 gear ratio to flip up either zombies or treasure chests
  • Joystick for player movement between lanes
  • Attack button for zombie attack
  • IR sensor that detects a hand wave for treasure collection
  • LED indicators for lives (red hearts) and zombie health (blue lights)
  • Speed dial for difficulty adjustment
